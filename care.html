Pet Care Guide 🧼

Detailed everyday care for Dogs, Cats, Rabbits, and Birds — feeding, housing, grooming, exercise, and mental well-being.

General Care (All Pets)

Feeding Basics

Choose species-appropriate, high-quality food. Feed measured portions at regular times. Always provide fresh water. Never give chocolate, grapes, onions, garlic, xylitol, or avocado (toxic to birds).

Safe Environment

Keep cleaning products, medicines, toxic plants, and small objects out of reach. Provide a quiet resting place. Secure windows and balconies.

🐶 Dog Care

Feeding

Complete dog food according to age and size. Most adults do well with two meals daily. Measure food to prevent obesity. Fresh water always available.

Exercise & Mental Stimulation

30–90 minutes of activity daily depending on breed. Combine walks with play and training. Puzzle toys and obedience work tire the mind as well as the body.

Grooming

Brush according to coat type. Bathe only when needed with dog-safe shampoo. Check ears, trim nails, and brush teeth regularly.

Training & Socialization

Start basic training early using positive reinforcement. Socialize puppies carefully with people, places, and other dogs.

🐱 Cat Care

Feeding

Cats are obligate carnivores. High-quality wet + dry food works well for many. Measured meals prevent obesity. Some cats prefer running water.

Litter Box

One box per cat + one extra. Scoop daily. Most cats prefer unscented clumping litter in a quiet location.

Enrichment

Vertical space (cat trees), daily interactive play, scratching posts, and puzzle feeders prevent boredom and behavior problems.

Grooming

Weekly brushing for short hair, daily for long hair. Most cats rarely need baths. Check ears and nails as needed.

🐰 Rabbit Care

Diet (Critical)

Unlimited grass hay (timothy/orchard) should be the majority of the diet. Small amount of pellets + daily leafy greens. Limit fruits and sugary treats.

Housing & Exercise

Large pen or rabbit-proofed room is better than a small cage. Several hours of supervised free-roam time daily. Provide tunnels and hiding places.

Grooming & Health

Brush regularly, especially during shedding. Never bathe unless advised by a vet. Check teeth — overgrown teeth are painful and common.

🐦 Bird Care

Diet

High-quality pellets + fresh vegetables + limited seeds/fruits. Seeds alone are not balanced. Avoid avocado, chocolate, caffeine, and high-salt foods.

Housing

Largest practical cage with horizontal bars. Different perch sizes and materials. Place in a social area away from drafts and kitchen fumes.

Social Needs & Enrichment

Most pet birds need daily interaction. Provide foraging and chewing toys. Rotate toys. Supervised out-of-cage time is beneficial.

Health Monitoring

Watch droppings, appetite, feather condition, and activity. Birds hide illness — early veterinary care is important.

Everyday Care Tips for All Pets

1

Establish a consistent daily routine — pets feel more secure with predictability.

2

Check body, eyes, ears, mouth, and skin at least once a week.

3

Reward good behavior with praise or healthy treats instead of punishment.

4

Keep a basic pet first-aid kit and the number of the nearest emergency vet.

5

Introduce new foods, people, or environments gradually.

6

Spend quality time with your pet every day.
